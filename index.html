Meet Our Doctor

Dr. Prashant Sharma

MBBS, MD (Medicine)

Consultant Physician and Assistant Professor with expertise in Internal Medicine and comprehensive management of adult medical disorders. He holds an MBBS, MD (Medicine), CPCDM and MIMA, with professional experience at reputed institutions

  • Formerly at AIIMS, New Delhi
  • Formerly at Safdarjung Hospital, New Delhi
  • Formerly at MMG Hospital, Ghaziabad

He provides evidence-based consultation for diabetes, hypertension, thyroid disorders, cardiovascular and respiratory diseases, infections, neurological conditions, lifestyle-related disorders and other acute and chronic medical conditions. His approach focuses on accurate diagnosis, rational treatment, preventive healthcare and personalized long-term management, with an emphasis on compassionate and patient-centred care.
